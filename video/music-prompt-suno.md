# Background Music — Suno Prompt

Generate a ~60-second **instrumental** background bed for the explainer (no vocals).

### Style / tags
`corporate, technology, ambient, uplifting, clean, minimal, instrumental, no vocals, soft piano, warm synth pads, subtle pulse, hopeful, trustworthy`

### Prompt
> Calm, modern, uplifting corporate-tech instrumental. Soft piano with warm synth pads and a gentle pulse; light, subtle percussion that builds just slightly toward the end. Hopeful, reassuring, and clean — the feeling of recovering something precious and getting it right. Minimal and unobtrusive so a voice-over sits clearly on top. No vocals. ~60 seconds, tempo around 90 BPM, key of C major.

### Production notes
- Make it **instrumental only** (so the English narration stays clear).
- Target length **60s** (or loopable).
- Keep it **quiet under the voice** — in the final mix the music sits at about **-18 to -22 dB** under the narration, with a gentle fade-in (0–2s) and fade-out (last 3s).
- Export as MP3/WAV and drop it at `video/assets/bg-music.mp3` (or hand me the file) for the final ffmpeg mix.
