### Single-block version (for one TTS call)

> Upgrading your Android phone should feel effortless. You run Smart Switch, everything looks like it copied over — and then the gaps appear. In WhatsApp, your documents, photos and videos are still listed. But tap one, and it says: file not found. The message survived — the file didn't. Phone Media Recovery solves this with one strict rule: match files by their exact path and name — never by a guess, a thumbnail, or a similar size. It scans both phones and compares them, folder by folder. In this real case, it found over eight thousand files that simply never made the jump. Then it restores only what's missing — byte for byte — through a safe, on-device archive transfer. Nothing is overwritten. Nothing is deleted. Every single file is checked by size and checksum, with its original date restored. If even one byte is off, it stops and tells you. Trustworthy, additive, Android-to-Android recovery. Your memories — back where they belong.
